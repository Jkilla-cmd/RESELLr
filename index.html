Bookmarklet: Import from Mercari & eBay

Drag the button below to your bookmarks bar. While viewing a listing on Mercari or eBay, click it — the title and price get copied to your clipboard. Then come back here (or the Inventory page) and click Paste Listing to drop it straight into the Add Item form.

+ Add to Comix Stash
  1. Show your browser's bookmarks bar (Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+B).
  2. Drag the gold + Add to Comix Stash button up into the bookmarks bar.
  3. Open a listing on Mercari or eBay, then click the bookmark.
  4. Come back to Comix Stash and click Paste Listing — the title, price, and platform will pre-fill the Add Item form. Fill in your cost and save.

Scraping depends on each site's page structure, so double-check the title and price before saving. If your browser blocks clipboard access, click Paste Listing and paste the copied text manually when prompted.
